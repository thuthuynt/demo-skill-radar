Definitions

In scope. Office jobs in every industry plus manual labour, from daily crawls. Technical, construction and specialist manufacturing jobs are not yet included.

Open now. Postings each site lists as open on its latest crawl day, with a closing date not yet passed. Closed postings are missing from this snapshot.

Skill match. A reviewed dictionary of 159 skills with Vietnamese and English names. Short abbreviations (BA, PM) match exactly; benefit sections and degree lines are skipped.

Archive samples. Postings saved by the Internet Archive, sampled evenly per month. Used for shares over time only, never for counts.
